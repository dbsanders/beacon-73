A note from the developer

From one ham to another.

Hi, I’m Bret, K6BAT. I built Beacon 73 because I wanted APRS beaconing on the iPhone to feel clear, approachable, and dependable in everyday use. It’s an independent project shaped by time on the air and feedback from other operators. I hope it makes your next outing a little easier—and I’m always glad to hear from a fellow ham.
